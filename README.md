## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.

• Provides logistics support.

• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.

• Lower prices for consumers.

• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
The proposed solution for this problem from my side is to build an AI-powered agricultural supply-chain management platform called CropBridge that connects farmers with buyers, warehouses, cold storage facilities and transportation providers.
The main objective is to reduce agricultural supply-chain inefficiencies, minimize post-harvest losses and help farmers find suitable markets for their products.
The platform allows farmers to upload crop details, available quantities, harvesting dates and locations. Based on this information, the system helps identify nearby buyers, storage facilities and transportation options.
Artificial Intelligence will be used to predict market demand, suggest suitable markets and support efficient distribution planning.

Key Features

1. Farmer Registration          :  Farmers can register and upload their crop details, quantities and harvesting information.
2. Nearby Market Finder         :  Helps farmers identify potential buyers and nearby markets for their products.
3. Smart Storage Locator        :  Displays nearby warehouses and cold storage facilities based on location and availability information.
4. AI-Based Demand Prediction   :  Predicts demand for different agricultural products using historical market data.
5. Smart Transport Management   :  Connects farmers with transportation providers and helps identify efficient delivery routes.
6. Crop Availability Tracking   :  Displays available crop quantities and updates product availability.
7. Post-Harvest Loss Prevention :  Provides alerts and recommendations to help farmers prioritize the distribution of perishable products.
8. Market Recommendation        :  Suggests suitable markets based on crop type, expected price, distance and transportation cost.

The proposed solution aims to improve agricultural distribution, reduce unnecessary transportation expenses and minimize post-harvest losses.

## Technical Approach
CropBridge will be developed as a web-based agricultural supply-chain application using Python, database technologies, Artificial Intelligence and digital mapping services.


○ Technologies Used

 • Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript
 
 • Backend: Python (Flask)
 
 • Database: MySQL
 
 • AI/ML: Python, Pandas and Scikit-learn
 
 • Maps and Routes: OpenStreetMap or Google Maps API
 

○ Implementation Steps

 Step 1 – Farmer Registration
 Farmers create accounts and provide their location and agricultural details.

 Step 2 – Crop Information Upload
 Farmers enter crop names, available quantities, harvesting dates and expected selling prices.

 Step 3 – Market Identification
 The system identifies potential buyers and nearby markets based on crop availability and location.

 Step 4 – AI-Based Demand Prediction
 Machine learning models analyse historical market data and identify demand patterns for different crops.

 Step 5 – Storage Facility Identification
 The platform helps farmers find suitable nearby storage facilities and cold storage options.

 Step 6 – Transportation Management
 The system helps identify transportation providers and calculate efficient delivery routes.

 Step 7 – Product Distribution
 Farmers can select suitable buyers and arrange transportation for their products.

 Step 8 – Tracking and Notifications
 The platform provides updates about product availability, orders and delivery status.
 

AI Implementation

 • Recommending suitable markets.
 
 • Identifying efficient delivery routes using route optimization algorithms.
 
 • Prioritizing distribution of perishable products.

## Feasibility and Viability
The platform provides a simple interface where farmers can upload crop information and find suitable markets, storage facilities and transportation options.
Regional language support and mobile-friendly design can improve accessibility.

Economic Feasibility

The initial prototype can be developed using open-source software and freely available development tools.
The project can initially operate without owning warehouses or transportation vehicles by connecting users with existing service providers.


○ Business Viability

The platform can generate revenue through:

 • Partnerships with warehouses and cold storage providers.
 
 • Logistics service commissions.
 
 • Subscription plans for bulk buyers.
 
 • Premium market-analysis services.
 
 • Partnerships with FPOs and agricultural organizations.
